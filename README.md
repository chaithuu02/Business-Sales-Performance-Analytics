Business Sales Performance Analytics
📌 Project Overview

Business Sales Performance Analytics is a data analytics project that focuses on analyzing sales data to uncover business insights, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and support data-driven decision-making. The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), sales trend analysis, customer segmentation, and dashboard creation.

By leveraging Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI, this project helps businesses understand revenue patterns, identify top-performing products, analyze customer behavior, and improve overall sales performance.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze sales performance across products, customers, and regions.
Identify revenue and profit trends.
Discover top-performing products and categories.
Understand customer purchasing behavior.
Generate actionable business insights.
Build interactive dashboards for business decision-making.
